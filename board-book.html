silo
A story about a little robot
and a safe place to play.
Robot wants to help you build things on your computer. It can read your files and type commands!
But sometimes Robot makes a big mess.
We tried giving Robot a big list of rules. “Don’t touch this! Don’t touch that!” But there were too many rules to remember.
Robot tried a sandbox instead, but it was too small for them, and sand kept falling out the sides.
So we built Robot a YES room, where Robot has no rules, and can do whatever it wants.
Type one little word and the YES room is ready. Robot is safe. Your computer is safe. Everyone is happy!
Robot has a cozy room to play in, and your computer stays nice and tidy. Goodnight, Robot!
